Execution-order item 9, second half. Writes data/scripts/export_nn_model.py,
mirroring export_model.py, plus Gate-1 revalidation of the exact exported
configuration and torch-vs-numpy parity tests.

DEV-94 hands it two HARD constraints. Getting either wrong ships an artifact
whose served explanation describes a different model:

  * The artifact must serialize ALL FIVE ensemble members. The selected config
    is SeedEnsemble(random_state=42, n_members=5, dropout=0.5,
    weight_decay=1e-2), hidden 64->32, members seeded random_state+i, rest at
    NNClassifier defaults. Read it from `selected_specification` in
    data/training/round2_results.json rather than retyping it.

  * IG EXPLAINS THE LOGIT OF THE MEAN PROBABILITY,
    g_c = log(mean_i softmax(z_i)_c) / T.  Therefore:
      - fit `temperature` on ENSEMBLE-AVERAGED OOF probabilities (that is
        export_model.py's deployment-temperature use of temperature_scale --
        ADR 0004 sanctions it for exactly this), and
      - revalidate Gate 1 on SeedEnsemble.predict_proba, i.e. the mean of the
        members' probabilities.
    Do NOT revalidate mean-of-members'-logits. mean(softmax(z)) is not
    softmax(mean(z)), and the whole DEV-94 attribution rests on which one.
    ADR 0004's per-outer-fold cross-fitting still governs any REPORTED ECE;
    the shipped constant is the pooled deployment one. Two different fits.

The artifact schema matcher_nn.py already reads and validates (see
services/matching/tests/conftest.py::nn_artifact for a worked example):
    model_type: "probability_averaged_mlp_ensemble"
    feature_version, feature_names, careers
    scaler_mean, scaler_scale        (ONE shared scaler -- every member
                                      standardizes on the same training stats)
    activation: "relu"               (anything else is refused at load)
    members: [ {"layers": [{"weight": [[..]], "bias": [..]}, ...]}, ... ]
                                      weight is (out_features, in_features),
                                      same convention as the linear artifact's
                                      `coef`
    temperature, label_source, caveats
Depth, widths and member count are all read from the artifact -- nothing is
hardcoded, so the exporter is free to change the shape.

Also owed by DEV-97: the torch-vs-numpy parity tests plan Step 5.2 asks for.
They were deliberately NOT done in DEV-94 -- backend/venv has neither torch nor
a trained artifact, so they belong in the training venv alongside the exporter.

2. THE OPEN DECISION I COULD NOT MAKE -- NEEDS A HUMAN
================================================================
THE IG STEP CAP IS SHORT -- BUT BY LESS THAN DEV-94 COULD TELL.

MEASURED ON THE REAL ARTIFACT (DEV-97, 2026-08-03), which is what section
2 below asked for and could not do:

    cd services/matching && ../../backend/venv/Scripts/python         tests/ig_diagnostics.py --artifact ../../data/models/matcher_nn_v1.json

    640 explanations : 75 fell through = 11.7%, strict median 5.17e-4
    1600 explanations: 167 fell through = 10.4%, strict median 5.13e-4

DEV-94's synthetic 60.9% was pessimistic by roughly a factor of five, and
its prediction that a weight_decay=1e-2 network would behave better was
right. The strict median now sits UNDER the 1e-3 tolerance rather than
above it. "A large minority of careers" is no longer the right
description of the shipped model; "about one in nine" is.

THE DECISION IS STILL A HUMAN'S -- it is resized, not settled. The three
options below are unchanged as options.

The integrand jumps at every ReLU breakpoint, so the midpoint Riemann sum is
O(1/m), not O(1/m^2). At the plan's tolerance (relative completeness residual
< 1e-3, measured against the centered delta being explained) and its cap of 512
steps, a large minority of careers emit NO model-derived reasons and fall
through to the non-model wording -- 60.9% on the measured fixture, 33.9% when
the same fixture's random weights are scaled by 0.3. The DIRECTION is robust,
the PERCENTAGE is not; quote the direction.

Beating O(1/m) requires locating the breakpoints, which is the analytic
activation-pattern tracking plan Step 5.2 explicitly rejects. So this is a real
accuracy / latency / tolerance trade, not a bug. The options are:
    - raise the cap (costs latency: ~200 ms/request today at TOP_N=3 and m=512;
      roughly linear in m),
    - loosen the tolerance, or change "relative" to mean relative to the
      attribution mass rather than the delta (Attribution.attribution_mass is
      carried so this is one line to evaluate -- at that reading essentially
      nothing falls through at either weight scale), or
    - accept the fall-through as designed ("better silence than attributions
      that do not sum to what they claim").

(DONE: measured above. ig_diagnostics.py now takes --artifact; with no
--artifact it still reproduces the DEV-94 synthetic table exactly, which is
what proves the change measures the artifact rather than moving goalposts.)

Full write-up: docs/dev-23-nn-rework-plan.md Step 5.2, and the DEV-94
